# Retro — v0.3.0, the top shelf (2026-10-20)

**Scope:** 0.2.0 → 0.3.0. The tvOS-style backdrop, and the focus lean and shine.

## What went well

- **Tuning by screenshot.** Each parameter (blur size, art alpha) was set by looking
  at the real TV, not by guessing: 24×14 still spelled NETFLIX; 0.55 washed the
  screen pale.
- **Saying no to the half version.** A Plex-only *Continue watching* row was buildable.
  The owner turned it down because it couldn't cover every app, which also kept their
  Plex token off the parents' TV.

## What was hard to understand

- **What "top shelf" can mean without content access.** Apple's shelf is the apps'
  own content. Every route to that on Android goes through a system-only permission,
  so the design had to change to a backdrop, not a feature that could be cut down.
